Lab notes

Ideas start small here, and we keep the ones people keep. These are short notes on what we shipped, changed and learned along the way, newest first.

  1. 23 October 2026

    Our YouTube channel is now Steadyline Labs

    The channel is now @steadylinelabs, so videos for every app live in one place. The Tether Link Utility how-to videos are already there.

  2. 23 October 2026

    Bil At! gets its first updates from player feedback

    The first players told us what felt off, so the next updates fix it. A reward now takes one ad, and the replay button and arrow counter are easier to read.

  3. 23 October 2026

    Tether Link Utility: one-file Windows setup

    Windows now has a setup file, so every app on the PC can use the phone's connection, not just the browser. The guide has new Windows and Mac videos.

  4. 23 October 2026

    The little one in the lab card grew up a bit

    The animated icon in the "Coming from the lab" card on the home page moved to its next stage. It keeps growing as the app does.

  5. 22 October 2026

    Bil At! is live on Google Play

    Bil At! is a quick-fire trivia game in Turkish: throw an arrow at a spinning board, and the number you hit picks the question. It went from first build to the store in under four weeks.

  6. 9 October 2026

    Fathom Pulse has a launch date

    Fathom Pulse comes to Android and iOS on January 1, 2027. We would rather take the extra time than rush it.

  7. 1 October 2026

    Tether Link Utility free plan: one device

    The free plan now shares your phone's data with one device at a time. Pro still covers up to five.

  8. 27 September 2026

    tetherlinkutility.com in five languages

    The Tether Link Utility site now reads in English, Spanish, Hindi, Portuguese and Turkish. The app itself stays in English and Turkish.

  9. 26 September 2026

    steadylinelabs.com is up

    One home for everything the lab makes. Tether Link Utility is on Android, with iOS on the way.

  10. 16 September 2026

    Tether Link Utility gets its own website

    tetherlinkutility.com went live with setup guides and plain posts on how carrier hotspot limits work.
