  3. 13 November 2026Update

    Bil At! 1.4.7: Alp and Banu, a sharper Ok Meydanı, daily gifts, ranks, a shop and more

    Bil At! 1.4.7 is out on Google Play now, and it carries everything we kept for this release. The App Store version is coming soon. Here is the full list.

    • Two archers. Pick Alp or Banu in Profile. Both are redrawn, front and back, with a medallion, a scroll, an earned badge and an archer emblem on the plate.
    • Ok Meydanı, much sharper. The archer turns to aim and draws a curved bow, and the arrow always leaves from the same spot. The plate stands on a pole and waves in the wind. The aim ring stays small and shows only direction, and in still air the arrow lands inside it. In Today’s Range every hit scores at once. The scope is now called Aim help and shows one red landing point.
    • A new Ok Meydanı for everyone. A chapter map that takes you through the ages, treasure chests to open, master duels to win and bow upgrades to earn along the way.
    • Daily tasks and gifts. Three tasks every day and a free daily gift, with the gift or the free tries left shown right on the Today tile.
    • Two rank paths. A Knowledge path and an Archery path, each with its own titles, a medallion and a new rank screen.
    • A shop. Boards and looks to choose from, with a new coin drawn by hand and used everywhere.
    • City weather in the game. Optional: rain, snow and storm can show up where you play.
    • Redrawn main menu. Logo and settings on top, your title progress and wallet below, the tagline and archer on stage, and Today, Tasks and Weekly ranking tiles under the cards. Titles fit on two lines and progress bars fill cleanly.
    • Leaderboards. Today’s Range and Archer of the Week have their own boards on Play Games and Game Center, and the ranking is fresh every time you open it.
    • Easier continues. A Double it button with a fixed size, a continue counter, and one more joker to use.
    • Smoother first launch, plus a cloud backup that never lowers your progress.

  13. 28 October 2026Update

    Bil At! 1.3.0: Ok Meydanı, a 3D archery range on the steppe

    Bil At! 1.3.0 adds a second mode next to the trivia game: Ok Meydanı (“the archery field”). It is three rounds of archery on a 3D steppe: fixed targets (a tower of water jugs, a gourd and a puta, no wind), then swinging targets (hanging jugs, a sliding sled, lanterns and a far puta, in a strong wind), then flying plates and jugs until the arrows run out, in a light wind. You can play it by day or by night.

    The camera sits behind the archer and moves in as you draw, so you aim with the arrow tip. A ribbon on the bow shows the wind. At full draw, time slows for a moment, a breath (nefes); hold it too long and the archer's arm tires. Hit a water jug and it topples and shatters.

    Also in 1.3.0: a new main menu with both modes, a faster first launch and bug fixes.
