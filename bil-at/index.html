Bil At!

A quick-fire trivia game in Turkish. Pick Alp or Banu, play for ranks, daily gifts and shop items.

  • New: Alp and Banu
  • Android
  • iOS
  • Turkish only
Get it on Google Play ↗ Download on the App Store ↗ bilat.app ↗

Bil At! is a free game in Turkish with two modes, side by side in the main menu, and two archers to play as, Alp and Banu. The first is a trivia quiz with a twist: you throw an arrow at a spinning board before each question. Pull back and let go, and the number you hit picks the difficulty. 1 to 4 is easy, 5 to 8 is medium, 9 to 12 is hard.

Then pick the right answer out of four within 20 seconds. A correct answer scores the number times ten. There are more than 1,400 questions across 8 categories, from general knowledge and history to cinema and food, and every three correct answers the board speeds up.

The second mode, added in version 1.3.0, is Ok Meydanı: three rounds of archery on a 3D steppe, with no questions. Draw the bow, read the wind from the ribbon on the bow and knock down towers of clay jugs. Fixed targets first, then swinging targets in a strong wind, then flying plates and jugs until the arrows run out, by day or by night.

Weekly and all-time leaderboards need only a nickname, no account. Downloaded questions stay on your phone, so you can play both modes offline.

What's new in 1.4.7

  • Two archers: pick Alp or Banu in your profile.
  • A new Ok Meydanı for every player: a chapter map that takes you through the ages, treasure chests to open, master duels to win and bow upgrades to earn.
  • Ok Meydanı keeps its wind, breath and three rounds, and now counts toward your archery rank.
  • Three new daily tasks every day and a daily gift on a 7-day streak, paid in üzerlik, the in-game coin.
  • Two rank paths with 12 ranks each: the Knowledge Path moves with every correct answer, the Archery Path with every hit in Ok Meydanı. Some ranks unlock board designs, feather and tip colours.
  • A shop for board designs, feather and tip colours and in-run helpers, paid with üzerlik earned in play.
  • Optional city weather: turn it on in Settings, pick your province, and rain, snow or storm shows up in the game.
